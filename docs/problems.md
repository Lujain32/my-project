# Three Candidate Problems

## Problem 1: Manual Gym Attendance & Member Tracking
* **Who has it:** Local gym owners and daily gym members.
* **How they cope today:** They use paper sign-in sheets every day. Members must manually sign their names upon arrival, and payments, weight tracking, and memberships are tracked manually on paper, leading to lost records and inefficiency.

## Problem 2: Scattered & Paid Access to Study/Training Scholarships (Chosen)
* **Who has it:** IT and AI students and fresh graduates looking for fully-funded or partially-funded scholarships and training opportunities abroad.
* **How they cope today:** Most existing platforms charge fees for information that is already publicly available. Students end up searching across dozens of unorganized websites (Telegram, LinkedIn, portals), keeping their application documents scattered across random local folders, and losing track of deadlines.

## Problem 3: Hospital & Clinic Appointment Scheduling
* **Who has it:** Patients and administrative staff at small hospitals and private clinics.
* **How they cope today:** Phone calls and paper logbooks. Patients struggle with double-booked time slots, changing clinic hours, and long wait times without live status updates.
