# Prompt Log (v1 to v4)

## Version 1
> "I am choosing a project to build over the next 8 weeks. Do not propose anything yet. Ask me one question at a time until you understand: the problem, who has it, what they do today instead, and what would count as solved. Stop and wait for my answer after each question. When you have enough, summarise it back to me in my own words and tell me what you still do not know."
* **What changed & why:** This was the initial prompt used to start the interrogation process and let the model explore the idea step by step.

## Version 2
> "The problem is that students and recent graduates searching for scholarships (whether fully or partially funded) or internships waste a tremendous amount of time navigating dozens of scattered websites. To make matters worse, most existing sites charge fees for information that is actually available for free on official university websites. Furthermore, students often lose track of their application files, motivation letters, and CVs across various locations, leading to a disorganized mess."
* **What changed & why:** Refined the problem definition in plain terms, splitting the pain points between discovery and document tracking.

## Version 3
> "I’ve been looking for scholarships to pursue a master’s degree or an internship abroad in my field (Artificial Intelligence), but I’ve found it incredibly difficult to gather the necessary information. The websites offering such details either charge a fee or present the information in a complicated way, and the documents I prepare—like motivation letters and the like—just get lost in the clutter on my computer."
* **What changed & why:** Narrowed down the target user persona to an AI graduate/student looking specifically for master's programs or internships abroad, based on personal experience.

## Version 4
> "Yes, dropping automated deadline alerts is a deliberate cut to keep the project strictly within the 8-week limit. For the chatbot, we can use a free-tier API to keep it at zero cost. Let's lock this scope down so we can move forward!"
* **What changed & why:** Locked down the final scope, explicitly cutting out automated deadlines and paid features to match the strict 8-week and zero-cost constraints.
